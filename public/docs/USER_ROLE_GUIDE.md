# DO-DEEL CDS Manager: User Role & Permissions Guide

Welcome to the **DO-DEEL CDS Manager**, the official digital accountability and growth platform for the Digital Onboarders CDS, Ondo State NYSC Directorate. This guide outlines the specific responsibilities, access levels, and operational workflows for each user role within the ecosystem.

---

## 1. CDS Coordinator (State Directorate)
**The Supreme Administrator.** Responsible for overall governance and strategic oversight.

*   **Permissions:** Highest level (Level 10).
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **System Control:** Access to the **Administration Control** panel to manage user roles, Local Government (LG) chapters, and system settings.
    *   **Data Integrity:** Can "Reset Sample Data" or "Clear All Data" for maintenance.
    *   **State-Wide Oversight:** View all members, tasks, activities, and reports across all 20 LGs.
    *   **Task Management:** Assign, edit, delete, or reopen any responsibility state-wide.
    *   **Content Publishing:** Upload system documents and publish learning resources.

---

## 2. State President
**The Executive Leader.** Directs operational synergy and capacity building across the state.

*   **Permissions:** Executive Leadership (Level 9).
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **Operational Control:** Assign responsibilities to any member or executive in the state.
    *   **State Dashboard:** Access the **Leadership Dashboard** with aggregated metrics for the entire state.
    *   **Accountability Oversight:** Monitor the **Accountability Engine** to ensure VPs and LG Presidents are meeting targets.
    *   **Report Review:** Review monthly reports from all Local Governments.
    *   **Resource Management:** Manage the Document Library and Learning Hub.

---

## 3. Vice Presidents (Accountability, Growth, Impact)
**Specialized Functional Leaders.** Oversee specific operational pillars.

*   **Permissions:** Functional Leadership (Level 8).
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **VP Accountability:** Focus on the **Accountability Engine**. Authorized to reopen tasks if evidence is insufficient and monitor state-wide deadlines.
    *   **VP Growth:** Focus on the **Growth Dashboard**. Authorized to publish new learning resources and manage statewide engagement activities.
    *   **VP Impact:** Focus on community-facing results and beneficiary tracking across all LGs.
    *   **Tasking:** Can assign tasks related to their specific functional area.

---

## 4. LG President
**Regional Operations Lead.** Manages CDS activities within a specific Local Government.

*   **Permissions:** Regional Leadership (Level 7).
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **Regional Scope:** Strictly scoped to their specific LG. They only see members and activities belonging to their chapter.
    *   **Member Enrollment:** Register and onboard new corps members into their LG chapter.
    *   **Attendance Management:** Mark attendance for LG-specific CDS meetings and activities.
    *   **Monthly Reporting:** Submit the official Monthly Operational Report (auto-aggregated by the system).
    *   **Local Tasking:** Assign responsibilities to members within their LG.

---

## 5. Executive Officer / Group Leader
**Operational Facilitators.** Support the execution of projects and member engagement.

*   **Permissions:** Operational (Level 4-5).
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **Execution:** View the **Executive Dashboard** to track their own assigned responsibilities.
    *   **Attendance Roll-Call:** Authorized to mark attendance for activities they are facilitating.
    *   **Reporting:** Assist in gathering data for monthly reports.
    *   **Member Support:** View member details within their assigned team or group.

---

## 6. Member
**The Digital Onboarder.** Executes community impact tasks and builds digital skills.

*   **Permissions:** Basic (Level 1).
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **Personal Dashboard:** Track personal responsibilities, deadlines, and attendance history.
    *   **Evidence Submission:** Submit text, images, or links as evidence for completed tasks.
    *   **Learning Hub:** Access YouTube tutorials, PDFs, and resources published by leadership.
    *   **Profile Management:** Update PPA details, skills, and contact information.

---

## Core System Workflows

### A. The Accountability Engine (Task Lifecycle)
1.  **Assignment:** Leadership assigns a task with a KPI and Deadline.
2.  **Execution:** Member moves task to "In Progress".
3.  **Completion:** Member submits Evidence and marks as "Completed".
4.  **Verification:** VP Accountability reviews. If evidence is poor, they **Reopen** the task with a reason.

### B. Monthly Reporting Flow
The system automatically tracks all completed tasks and attendance records for each month. At the end of the month, the **LG President** generates the report, which is pre-filled with this data, and submits it for State Review.

### C. Learning & Growth
Members earn badges and progress through the **Growth Hub** by participating in activities and completing digital literacy resources in the **Learning Hub**.
